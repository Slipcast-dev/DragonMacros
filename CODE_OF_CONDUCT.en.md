# Code of conduct

[Русский](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md)

Respect contributors, discuss solutions on their merits, and accommodate different
levels of experience. Constructive criticism, reproducible reports, and help for
newcomers are welcome. Insults, discrimination, threats, harassment, spam, and
publication of other people's personal information are not acceptable. These rules
apply to project issues, pull requests, and discussions.

The owner, @Slipcast-dev, reviews violations and may remove content, close a discussion,
or restrict participation in proportion to the violation. For a non-public report,
use a contact voluntarily published on the [owner's profile](https://github.com/Slipcast-dev).
Do not publish personal data or vulnerability details in a public report.
