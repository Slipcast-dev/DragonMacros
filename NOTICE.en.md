`Icon/source/dragon-logo-original.png` and `docs/images/hero.png` were created on
2026-10-30 with OpenAI's built-in `image_gen` tool for Dragon Macros.
The new `Icon/source/japan-app-icon.png` — a Japanese sun and ink stroke — was created
on 2026-11-14 with the same tool, prepared with transparency, and approved by the user
for version 0.5. This is AI-generated artwork, not a claim of hand-drawn illustration.
Sources and derived resources are described in [docs/ARTWORK.en.md](docs/ARTWORK.en.md).

The owner distributes these materials and derivatives under MIT to the extent of
the rights they hold. Exclusivity and copyright protection for AI-generated artwork
are not guaranteed.

No third-party artwork is used in the project. The version 0.5 app icon is prepared
from `japan-app-icon.png` by `Icon/make_icons.py`. The menu bar icon, a dragon in a
circle, and archived emblems derive from the project's own logo; their initial
preparation uses `Icon/prepare_logo.py`. The native window background, including its
red disk and abstract lines, is drawn with AppKit/Core Animation.

`docs/images/hero-samurai.png` was created on 2026-11-14 with OpenAI's built-in
`image_gen`, using the approved icon as reference: a graphite background, Japanese
sun and ink stroke, the Dragon Macros name, and the captions “Горячие клавиши для macOS” /
“Hotkeys for macOS”. It replaces previous artwork in the public READMEs; older
materials remain in the project history.
